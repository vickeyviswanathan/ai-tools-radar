2. SCAM ALERTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Fake Google Customer Care & Screen Share Hijack [Vector: Google Search / WhatsApp Video]
• How it works: When searching for Swiggy, Zomato, IRCTC, or bank customer support on Google, users dial a fake number placed by scammers. The fake agent says: "To process your ₹200 refund, share your screen on WhatsApp video call or install AnyDesk/QuickSupport." Once screen sharing starts, the scammer initiates a bank transfer and reads the incoming OTP directly off the live screen.
• Defense rule: Never search for customer service phone numbers on Google Search; always use the in-app help section. Never share your mobile screen or accept screen-sharing requests during a customer support call.
• Helpline: Dial 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) or report at https://cipherati.com/scam-alerts.html
