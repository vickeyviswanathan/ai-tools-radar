2. SCAM ALERTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Fake UPI Autopay / Mandate Approval Scam [Vector: UPI Autopay / Collect Request]
• How it works: Fraudsters pose as customer care, OTT/telecom billing teams, or marketplace buyers and send a UPI Autopay mandate request disguised as a 'refund approval', 'subscription cancellation', or 'KYC renewal'. When you enter your UPI PIN, you do not receive money—you authorise recurring automatic debits that quietly drain your bank account.
• Defense rule: Entering your UPI PIN is ALWAYS for paying or authorising a debit—never to receive a refund or cancel a plan. Regularly inspect the 'UPI Autopay / Mandates' section in your UPI app and revoke any unknown mandate.
• Full playbook: https://cipherati.com/scams/fake-upi-autopay-mandate-scam.html
• Helpline: Dial 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) or view all 31 alerts at https://cipherati.com/scam-alerts.html
