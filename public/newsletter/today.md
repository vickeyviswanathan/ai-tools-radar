## 🚨 Active Scam Watch
### **The "Digital Arrest" Police Video Call** (WhatsApp / Video Call)
* **The Trap**: A caller in uniform claims your Aadhaar or passport was found in an illegal parcel or money laundering probe. They place you on a fraudulent WhatsApp video call or custom video link with a backdrop resembling a police station, CBI, or ED cyber cell, declaring you are under 'Digital Arrest' and coercing you to transfer savings into a fake 'RBI verification escrow'.
* **The Defense Rule**: **Indian law and law enforcement agencies have NO legal concept of 'Digital Arrest'. Police, CBI, ED, and customs NEVER conduct interrogations, trials, or arrests over video calls. Hang up immediately, block the number, and report to the National Cybercrime Portal or dial 1930.**
* **Emergency Action**: If targeted, call **1930** (Toll-Free National Cyber Helpline) within the Golden Hour.
