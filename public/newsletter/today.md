The "Look-Alike Voice" AI Deepfake Distress Call [Vector: Phone Call / Audio AI]
• How it works: An elderly parent receives a call that sounds identically like their son or daughter crying in panic: "Dad, I was in a car crash / police detained me, please send ₹50,000 to this inspector's UPI immediately to avoid lockup." • Defense rule: Establish a family secret safe-word. Never transfer money under emotional pressure. Hang up immediately and dial your child's number directly on a normal voice call or video call.
