The Fake Electricity Bill Disconnection Warning [Vector: SMS / Remote App]
• How it works: You get an urgent SMS: "Dear consumer, your electricity connection will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid balance. Call power officer immediately." • Defense rule: Power boards never threaten same-night power cuts over SMS or ask you to call personal mobile numbers. Never install screen-sharing apps on advice from a stranger. Pay bills only via authorized electricity board portals.
