2. SCAM ALERTS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Cyber Fraud Recovery Agent Scam [Vector: Social Media / Phone Call]
• How it works: Fraudsters target people who have already lost money to a cyber scam—finding them through social media comments, fake 'cyber help' pages, or leaked complaint data—and pose as ethical hackers, cyber lawyers, RBI recovery officers, or special investigators. They claim they have traced your stolen ₹5 lakh and demand an advance 'server fee' or 'legal processing fee' to recover it, scamming the victim a second time.
• Defense rule: NO private hacker, lawyer, or 'recovery agent' on Instagram, Telegram, or WhatsApp can trace or reverse stolen funds outside official police and banking channels. Use ONLY the government's free 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in.
• Full playbook: https://cipherati.com/scams/cyber-fraud-recovery-agent-scam.html
• Helpline: Dial 1930 (National Cyber Helpline) or view all 31 alerts at https://cipherati.com/scam-alerts.html
