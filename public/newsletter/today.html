02 · SCAM ALERTS

The DoT / TRAI "Mobile Number Disconnection in 2 Hours" Robocall (Phone Call / Robocall IVR)

The pattern: An automated IVR robocall says: "This is the Department of Telecommunications (DoT). Your mobile number will be disconnected in 2 hours due to illegal cybercrime complaints. Press 9 to speak with an officer." Pressing 9 routes you to fake police who threaten immediate arrest under PMLA unless you pay money to verify your Aadhaar.

Defense rule: DoT and TRAI NEVER disconnect phone numbers via automated calls or ask you to press 9. If you receive such a call, hang up immediately. Check your registered mobile connections on the official Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in) portal or dial 1930.
