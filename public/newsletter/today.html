02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Airport Lounge & Credit Card Points Phishing Link → (SMS / Web Clone)

The pattern: You receive an SMS: "Your complimentary airport lounge access and reward points expire tonight. Re-verify your card benefits immediately: [shortened link]". The link opens a cloned DreamFolks/bank webpage asking for card number, CVV, expiry, and OTP, allowing fraudsters to make high-value international transactions.

Defense rule: Banks and airport lounge operators never send SMS links threatening sudden cancellation of lounge privileges. Never enter card CVV or OTPs on links received via SMS. Check card benefits directly in your bank's official app.
