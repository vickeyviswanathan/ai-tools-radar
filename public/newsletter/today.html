02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Fake UPI Autopay / Mandate Approval Scam → (UPI Autopay / Collect Request)

The pattern: Fraudsters pose as customer care, OTT/telecom billing teams, or marketplace buyers and send a UPI Autopay mandate request disguised as a 'refund approval', 'subscription cancellation', or 'KYC renewal'. When you enter your UPI PIN, you do not receive money—you authorise recurring automatic debits that quietly drain your bank account.

Defense rule: Entering your UPI PIN is ALWAYS for paying or authorising a debit—never to receive a refund or cancel a plan. Regularly inspect the 'UPI Autopay / Mandates' section in your UPI app and revoke any unknown mandate.
