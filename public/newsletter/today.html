02 · SCAM ALERTS

The WhatsApp Part-Time "Hotel Review / Like" Job (WhatsApp / Telegram)

The pattern: You get a WhatsApp message offering ₹2,000–₹5,000 per day to like YouTube videos or review hotels. You complete initial tasks and receive ₹300 genuine payout to build trust. Then you are added to a Telegram VIP group and convinced to deposit ₹50,000 to "unlock" higher commissions, which you can never withdraw.

Defense rule: Legitimate businesses never recruit over WhatsApp to pay for video likes. Any scheme paying small token amounts upfront to lure you into depositing larger "prepaid" funds is a scam.
