02 · SCAM ALERTS

The "Look-Alike Voice" AI Deepfake Distress Call (Phone Call / Audio AI)

The pattern: An elderly parent receives a call that sounds identically like their son or daughter crying in panic: "Dad, I was in a car crash / police detained me, please send ₹50,000 to this inspector's UPI immediately to avoid lockup." The voice is an AI clone generated from a 5-second public Instagram clip.

Defense rule: Establish a family secret safe-word. Never transfer money under emotional pressure. Hang up immediately and dial your child's number directly on a normal voice call or video call.
