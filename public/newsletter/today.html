02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Corporate Tax & MCA Notice Archive Trap (Infostealer ZIP) (Email / WhatsApp)

The pattern: Accountants, CAs, company directors, and finance personnel receive an urgent email or WhatsApp: "Statutory notice regarding pending tax filing discrepancies / MCA audit. Attached: Statement_of_Account.zip or MCA_Notice.zip." Extracting the file runs a disguised Windows executable that quietly exfiltrates corporate net banking tokens, DSC credentials, and browser passwords.

Defense rule: Tax authorities and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) never send audit notices as compressed .zip or .rar files via WhatsApp or personal email. Check all corporate filings directly on the official mca.gov.in portal.
