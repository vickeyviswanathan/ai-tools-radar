02 · SCAM ALERTS

The WhatsApp "Wedding Card" Virus (APK Trojan) (WhatsApp / Android APK)

The pattern: You receive a message from a friend or relative on WhatsApp saying: "My cousin is getting married next week, wedding card and venue attached." The attached file looks like an invite, but ends in .apk. Installing it infects your phone with a virus that hijacks your WhatsApp, sends the same scam to all your family contacts, and steals your net banking logins.

Defense rule: Real wedding cards are always photos or PDFs, never an .apk software file. If someone sends an APK file, do not tap it. Call that person on a regular call to tell them their WhatsApp is compromised.
