02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Fake Google Customer Care & Screen Share Hijack (Google Search / WhatsApp Video)

The pattern: When searching for Swiggy, Zomato, IRCTC, or bank customer support on Google, users dial a fake number placed by scammers. The fake agent says: "To process your ₹200 refund, share your screen on WhatsApp video call or install AnyDesk/QuickSupport." Once screen sharing starts, the scammer initiates a bank transfer and reads the incoming OTP directly off the live screen.

Defense rule: Never search for customer service phone numbers on Google Search; always use the in-app help section. Never share your mobile screen or accept screen-sharing requests during a customer support call.

Reality #6: The Structured JSON Illusion

Common myth: "Just tell the LLM to output valid JSON matching your schema — instant API integration!"

What works in production: Never rely on raw prompting for JSON. Use constrained grammar sampling at the model engine level (forcing logits to only output valid schema tokens).
