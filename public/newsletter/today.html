02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Romance-to-Investment / Pig Butchering Scam (Dating Apps / Fake Crypto & Trading)

The pattern: Fraudsters build a warm, patient online friendship or romantic relationship over weeks on dating apps, LinkedIn, or Instagram—often using stolen photos or AI-enhanced personas—before casually introducing a 'guaranteed' crypto, forex, or gold trading platform. Initial small withdrawals succeed to build trust, followed by massive losses when you deposit life savings and are blocked from withdrawing.

Defense rule: Never mix online romance or unverified social connections with financial investments. Verify any trading platform with SEBI before depositing funds, and treat any request to move from a dating app to WhatsApp/Telegram to 'invest together' as a major red flag.
