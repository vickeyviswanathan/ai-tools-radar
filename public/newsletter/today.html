02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Fake Electricity Bill Disconnection Warning (SMS / Remote App)

The pattern: You get an urgent SMS: "Dear consumer, your electricity connection will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid balance. Call power officer immediately." If you call, they tell you to install QuickSupport or AnyDesk to pay ₹11 for KYC update, which gives them full control to drain your bank account.

Defense rule: Power boards never threaten same-night power cuts over SMS or ask you to call personal mobile numbers. Never install screen-sharing apps on advice from a stranger. Pay bills only via authorized electricity board portals.
