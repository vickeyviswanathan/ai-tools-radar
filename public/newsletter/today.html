02 · SCAM ALERTS

The FASTag Highway Blacklist Trap → (SMS / Web Clone)

The pattern: You get an urgent SMS saying your FASTag is blacklisted or KYC is expired, demanding a ₹45 pending fee within 2 hours or your vehicle will be stopped at the next toll plaza. The link opens a fake cloned website that captures your bank account and debit card details.

Defense rule: Highway authorities never send SMS links to collect ₹45 or update KYC. Never click links in SMS. If you want to check your FASTag, open your own banking app or Paytm directly.
