02 · SCAM ALERTS

The Bogus Stock Market "VIP Institutional IPO" Group → (WhatsApp / Fake App)

The pattern: You are added to a WhatsApp group named after a famous global brokerage. "Mentors" promise guaranteed 100% allocation in blockbuster IPOs and instruct you to trade through a customized app. The app shows massive fake profits, but when you attempt to withdraw, they demand a 20% "tax release fee" and freeze your account.

Defense rule: SEBI-registered brokerages never conduct institutional IPO bidding or accept investments via WhatsApp groups. Never deposit money into personal accounts for stock market trading.
