🚨 Golden Hour Emergency Protocol (First 2 Hours)

1. Dial 1930 Immediately (Toll-Free): Trigger an inter-bank freeze on mule accounts.
2. File e-FIR: cybercrime.gov.in  ·  3. Report Scam Calls/SMS: sancharsaathi.gov.in (Chakshu)
4. Save Evidence: Uncropped screenshots of chats, 12-digit UPI UTR numbers, and bank SMS alerts.

🛡️ 12 Critical Scam Rules Every Employee & Family Member Must Know

The "Digital Arrest" Police Video Call Indian law and law enforcement agencies have NO legal concept of 'Digital Arrest'. Police, CBI, ED, and customs NEVER conduct interrogations, trials, or arrests over video calls. Hang up immediately, block the number, and report to the National Cybercrime Portal or dial 1930. Read Playbook → The Fake Traffic Fine Message (e-Challan APK) Traffic police never send app files to pay fines. Never install any file ending in .apk. Always check and pay traffic fines only on the official government website: echallan.parivahan.gov.in. Read Playbook → The WhatsApp "Wedding Card" Virus (APK Trojan) Real wedding cards are always photos or PDFs, never an .apk software file. If someone sends an APK file, do not tap it. Call that person on a regular call to tell them their WhatsApp is compromised. Read Playbook → The "Wrong UPI Transfer" Crying Call Never transfer the money back yourself. Tell the caller: "I will not touch this money. Please call your bank and file a wrong-transfer dispute. My bank will reverse it officially." Read Playbook → The Fake QR Code "To Receive Payment" Trap Remember the golden rule of UPI: You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money. Entering a PIN is only required when money leaves your bank account. Read Playbook → The "5G Plus Upgrade" Number Steal Never send an eSIM request to any email given by a caller. Telecom companies never call you to set up an eSIM. To upgrade your SIM, always visit an official Airtel/Jio store in person. Read Playbook → The FASTag Highway Blacklist Trap Highway authorities never send SMS links to collect ₹45 or update KYC. Never click links in SMS. If you want to check your FASTag, open your own banking app or Paytm directly. Read Playbook → The "Look-Alike Voice" AI Deepfake Distress Call Establish a family secret safe-word. Never transfer money under emotional pressure. Hang up immediately and dial your child's number directly on a normal voice call or video call. Read Playbook → The Fake Electricity Bill Disconnection Warning Power boards never threaten same-night power cuts over SMS or ask you to call personal mobile numbers. Never install screen-sharing apps on advice from a stranger. Pay bills only via authorized electricity board portals. Read Playbook → The WhatsApp Part-Time "Hotel Review / Like" Job Legitimate businesses never recruit over WhatsApp to pay for video likes. Any scheme paying small token amounts upfront to lure you into depositing larger "prepaid" funds is a scam. Read Playbook → The DoT / TRAI "Mobile Number Disconnection in 2 Hours" Robocall DoT and TRAI NEVER disconnect phone numbers via automated calls or ask you to press 9. If you receive such a call, hang up immediately. Check your registered mobile connections on the official Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in) portal or dial 1930. Read Playbook → The FedEx / DHL "Drugs Found in International Courier" Threat Courier companies like FedEx and DHL never transfer customer calls to police or narcotics agencies. Police never interrogate suspects or demand security deposits over video calls. Hang up, block, and dial 1930. Read Playbook →
