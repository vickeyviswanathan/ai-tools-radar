WhatsApp / Video Call Digital Arrest CRITICAL

The "Digital Arrest" Police Video Call

A caller in uniform claims your Aadhaar or passport was found in an illegal parcel or money laundering probe. They place you on a fraudulent WhatsApp video call or custom video link with a backdrop resembling a police station, CBI, or ED cyber cell, declaring you are under 'Digital Arrest' and coercing you to transfer savings into a fake 'RBI verification escrow'.

How to protect yourself:

Indian law and law enforcement agencies have NO legal concept of 'Digital Arrest'. Police, CBI, ED, and customs NEVER conduct interrogations, trials, or arrests over video calls. Hang up immediately, block the number, and report to the National Cybercrime Portal or dial 1930.

🏛️ I4C & PM Mann Ki Baat Cyber Alert
National Cyber Helpline: 1930 ↗
SMS / Android APK Traffic Challan APK CRITICAL

The Fake Traffic Fine Message (e-Challan APK)

You get an SMS claiming your vehicle was caught overspeeding on camera with a pending fine of ₹1,000, and a link to download an app (echallan.apk) to pay before court action. If you install it, the app silently reads all your incoming SMS, steals your bank OTPs, and drains your account.

How to protect yourself:

Traffic police never send app files to pay fines. Never install any file ending in .apk. Always check and pay traffic fines only on the official government website: echallan.parivahan.gov.in.

🏛️ I4C (Ministry of Home Affairs) Cyber Alert
Official Portal: echallan.parivahan.gov.in ↗
WhatsApp / Android APK Wedding Card Virus CRITICAL

The WhatsApp "Wedding Card" Virus (APK Trojan)

You receive a message from a friend or relative on WhatsApp saying: "My cousin is getting married next week, wedding card and venue attached." The attached file looks like an invite, but ends in .apk. Installing it infects your phone with a virus that hijacks your WhatsApp, sends the same scam to all your family contacts, and steals your net banking logins.

How to protect yourself:

Real wedding cards are always photos or PDFs, never an .apk software file. If someone sends an APK file, do not tap it. Call that person on a regular call to tell them their WhatsApp is compromised.

UPI / Phone Call Wrong UPI Transfer HIGH

The "Wrong UPI Transfer" Crying Call

You get a genuine bank alert that ₹30,000 was credited to your account. Seconds later, a crying person calls saying: "Sir, my child is in the hospital, I sent money to your number by mistake. Please send it back to my UPI ID." If you send the money back yourself, you lose your own money—because the original transfer was made using stolen funds. Within 48 hours, cyber police freeze your bank account for receiving criminal money.

How to protect yourself:

Never transfer the money back yourself. Tell the caller: "I will not touch this money. Please call your bank and file a wrong-transfer dispute. My bank will reverse it officially."

🏛️ Reserve Bank of India (RBI Sachet Advisory)
RBI Sachet Portal ↗
OLX / UPI QR Code QR Code Money Trap HIGH

The Fake QR Code "To Receive Payment" Trap

You list furniture or a vehicle for sale on OLX or Marketplace. A buyer immediately agrees to the price without negotiation and sends you a UPI QR code saying: "Scan this QR code and enter your UPI PIN to receive the advance payment of ₹15,000." The moment you enter your PIN, ₹15,000 is debited from your account.

How to protect yourself:

Remember the golden rule of UPI: You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to RECEIVE money. Entering a PIN is only required when money leaves your bank account.

🏛️ NPCI & National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
NPCI Official Portal ↗
eSIM / SMS eSIM Hijack CRITICAL

The "5G Plus Upgrade" Number Steal

A caller claiming to be from Airtel or Jio says they are upgrading your SIM to 5G for free. They tell you to send a simple SMS like eSIM <email> to 121. The moment you do this, your physical SIM card stops working permanently, and your phone number transfers to the scammer's phone. All your bank SMS OTPs now go to them.

How to protect yourself:

Never send an eSIM request to any email given by a caller. Telecom companies never call you to set up an eSIM. To upgrade your SIM, always visit an official Airtel/Jio store in person.

🏛️ DoT (Department of Telecommunications) / Sanchar Saathi
Sanchar Saathi Portal ↗
SMS / Web Clone FASTag Toll Alert HIGH

The FASTag Highway Blacklist Trap

You get an urgent SMS saying your FASTag is blacklisted or KYC is expired, demanding a ₹45 pending fee within 2 hours or your vehicle will be stopped at the next toll plaza. The link opens a fake cloned website that captures your bank account and debit card details.

How to protect yourself:

Highway authorities never send SMS links to collect ₹45 or update KYC. Never click links in SMS. If you want to check your FASTag, open your own banking app or Paytm directly.

🏛️ NPCI & Cyber Crime Coordination Centre
NPCI Official Portal ↗
Phone Call / Audio AI AI Voice Clone CRITICAL

The "Look-Alike Voice" AI Deepfake Distress Call

An elderly parent receives a call that sounds identically like their son or daughter crying in panic: "Dad, I was in a car crash / police detained me, please send ₹50,000 to this inspector's UPI immediately to avoid lockup." The voice is an AI clone generated from a 5-second public Instagram clip.

How to protect yourself:

Establish a family secret safe-word. Never transfer money under emotional pressure. Hang up immediately and dial your child's number directly on a normal voice call or video call.

SMS / Remote App Power Bill Alert HIGH

The Fake Electricity Bill Disconnection Warning

You get an urgent SMS: "Dear consumer, your electricity connection will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid balance. Call power officer immediately." If you call, they tell you to install QuickSupport or AnyDesk to pay ₹11 for KYC update, which gives them full control to drain your bank account.

How to protect yourself:

Power boards never threaten same-night power cuts over SMS or ask you to call personal mobile numbers. Never install screen-sharing apps on advice from a stranger. Pay bills only via authorized electricity board portals.

🏛️ Ministry of Power & I4C Advisory
National Cyber Helpline: 1930 ↗
WhatsApp / Telegram Job Task Fraud HIGH

The WhatsApp Part-Time "Hotel Review / Like" Job

You get a WhatsApp message offering ₹2,000–₹5,000 per day to like YouTube videos or review hotels. You complete initial tasks and receive ₹300 genuine payout to build trust. Then you are added to a Telegram VIP group and convinced to deposit ₹50,000 to "unlock" higher commissions, which you can never withdraw.

How to protect yourself:

Legitimate businesses never recruit over WhatsApp to pay for video likes. Any scheme paying small token amounts upfront to lure you into depositing larger "prepaid" funds is a scam.

🏛️ Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)
I4C Cybercrime Portal ↗
Phone Call / Robocall IVR DoT Disconnect Notice CRITICAL

The DoT / TRAI "Mobile Number Disconnection in 2 Hours" Robocall

An automated IVR robocall says: "This is the Department of Telecommunications (DoT). Your mobile number will be disconnected in 2 hours due to illegal cybercrime complaints. Press 9 to speak with an officer." Pressing 9 routes you to fake police who threaten immediate arrest under PMLA unless you pay money to verify your Aadhaar.

How to protect yourself:

DoT and TRAI NEVER disconnect phone numbers via automated calls or ask you to press 9. If you receive such a call, hang up immediately. Check your registered mobile connections on the official Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in) portal or dial 1930.

🏛️ DoT & Sanchar Saathi Advisory
Sanchar Saathi Official Portal ↗
Phone Call / Video Call Customs Drug Parcel CRITICAL

The FedEx / DHL "Drugs Found in International Courier" Threat

An automated call claiming to be FedEx or DHL claims: "A courier parcel sent using your Aadhaar to Taiwan or Cambodia containing MDMA drugs, fake passports, and illegal debit cards was intercepted by customs." The call is transferred to fake "Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)" or "Mumbai Crime Branch" officers over video call who demand financial statements and emergency escrow transfers.

How to protect yourself:

Courier companies like FedEx and DHL never transfer customer calls to police or narcotics agencies. Police never interrogate suspects or demand security deposits over video calls. Hang up, block, and dial 1930.

🏛️ I4C & Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) Advisory
National Cyber Helpline: 1930 ↗
Instagram / Facebook / YouTube Deepfake Welfare Ads HIGH

The Deepfake Government Welfare & PM Yojana Ads

Sponsored video ads on Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube feature hyper-realistic AI deepfakes of government leaders and ministers announcing instant "₹5,000 festive relief funds" or "free smartphone subsidies". Clicking the link directs you to an unauthorized portal that triggers a UPI Collect Request (which debits your account) or installs banking malware.

How to protect yourself:

Government welfare schemes are never disbursed via social media ads or UPI collect requests. Entering your UPI PIN always DEDUCTS money from your bank account. Verify official government schemes only on .gov.in portals.

🏛️ I4C National Cybercrime Threat Analytics Unit (NCTAU)
I4C Cybercrime Advisory ↗
Google Search / WhatsApp Video Screen Share Refund CRITICAL

The Fake Google Customer Care & Screen Share Hijack

When searching for Swiggy, Zomato, IRCTC, or bank customer support on Google, users dial a fake number placed by scammers. The fake agent says: "To process your ₹200 refund, share your screen on WhatsApp video call or install AnyDesk/QuickSupport." Once screen sharing starts, the scammer initiates a bank transfer and reads the incoming OTP directly off the live screen.

How to protect yourself:

Never search for customer service phone numbers on Google Search; always use the in-app help section. Never share your mobile screen or accept screen-sharing requests during a customer support call.

🏛️ Reserve Bank of India (RBI Sachet Advisory)
RBI Sachet Portal ↗
Email / WhatsApp Corporate Infostealer CRITICAL

The Corporate Tax & MCA Notice Archive Trap (Infostealer ZIP)

Accountants, CAs, company directors, and finance personnel receive an urgent email or WhatsApp: "Statutory notice regarding pending tax filing discrepancies / MCA audit. Attached: Statement_of_Account.zip or MCA_Notice.zip." Extracting the file runs a disguised Windows executable that quietly exfiltrates corporate net banking tokens, DSC credentials, and browser passwords.

How to protect yourself:

Tax authorities and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) never send audit notices as compressed .zip or .rar files via WhatsApp or personal email. Check all corporate filings directly on the official mca.gov.in portal.

🏛️ I4C Corporate Threat Advisory & CERT-In
CERT-In Advisory Desk ↗
Phone Call / WhatsApp Pensioner KYC Fraud HIGH

The Pensioner "Digital Life Certificate / Jeevan Pramaan" KYC Scam

Fraudsters call senior citizens posing as bank or pension disbursement officers: "Your annual digital life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) is expiring today. Your pension will stop next month unless verified right now." The caller asks for Aadhaar OTPs or asks them to install a remote-access app to "scan their face", instantly draining the pensioner's life savings.

How to protect yourself:

Pension authorities never call to ask for Aadhaar OTPs or banking passwords to submit Jeevan Pramaan. Senior citizens should submit life certificates only via the official Jeevan Pramaan Face App or in person at bank branches or post offices.

🏛️ Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions & I4C
National Cyber Helpline: 1930 ↗
SMS / Web Clone Lounge Pass Phishing HIGH

The Airport Lounge & Credit Card Points Phishing Link

You receive an SMS: "Your complimentary airport lounge access and reward points expire tonight. Re-verify your card benefits immediately: [shortened link]". The link opens a cloned DreamFolks/bank webpage asking for card number, CVV, expiry, and OTP, allowing fraudsters to make high-value international transactions.

How to protect yourself:

Banks and airport lounge operators never send SMS links threatening sudden cancellation of lounge privileges. Never enter card CVV or OTPs on links received via SMS. Check card benefits directly in your bank's official app.

CERT-In Advisory Desk ↗
SMS / Web Clone Courier Address Trap HIGH

The Fake Courier Delivery / Incomplete Address Trap

You receive an SMS from "India Post" or "Courier": "Your package cannot be delivered due to an incomplete house number. Update your address within 24 hours or parcel will be returned." The link opens an India Post cloned webpage asking for a ₹25 redelivery fee, harvesting your debit card number and OTP.

How to protect yourself:

India Post never sends SMS links asking for fee payments to correct addresses. Track parcels only using the tracking number directly on the official indiapost.gov.in website.

WhatsApp / Fake App Fake IPO Scheme CRITICAL

The Bogus Stock Market "VIP Institutional IPO" Group

You are added to a WhatsApp group named after a famous global brokerage. "Mentors" promise guaranteed 100% allocation in blockbuster IPOs and instruct you to trade through a customized app. The app shows massive fake profits, but when you attempt to withdraw, they demand a 20% "tax release fee" and freeze your account.

How to protect yourself:

SEBI-registered brokerages never conduct institutional IPO bidding or accept investments via WhatsApp groups. Never deposit money into personal accounts for stock market trading.
