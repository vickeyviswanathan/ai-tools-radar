The "Wrong UPI Transfer" Crying Call (Wrong UPI Transfer) — Modus Operandi & 1930 Protection Guide

Statutory citizen cyber defense advisory based on Reserve Bank of India (RBI Sachet Advisory) guidelines.

📱 Attack Vector: UPI / Phone Call 🏷️ Pattern: Wrong UPI Transfer 🏛️ Source: Reserve Bank of India (RBI Sachet Advisory)

🕵️ How the Scam Works (Modus Operandi)

⚠️ The Trap (UPI / Phone Call): You get a genuine bank alert that ₹30,000 was credited to your account. Seconds later, a crying person calls saying: "Sir, my child is in the hospital, I sent money to your number by mistake. Please send it back to my UPI ID." If you send the money back yourself, you lose your own money—because the original transfer was made using stolen funds. Within 48 hours, cyber police freeze your bank account for receiving criminal money.

🛡️ Mandatory Defense Rule

✅ How to Protect Yourself & Your Family: Never transfer the money back yourself. Tell the caller: "I will not touch this money. Please call your bank and file a wrong-transfer dispute. My bank will reverse it officially."

🚨 Emergency 4-Step Golden Hour Protocol (If Targeted)

  1. Dial 1930 Immediately (Golden Hour): Call the toll-free National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 within 2 hours of an unauthorized UPI/bank transfer to freeze the funds in the recipient mule account before ATM withdrawal.
  2. Disconnect Device if an APK Was Installed: If a file ending in .apk (e.g. e-Challan, Wedding Invite, KYC app) was installed, turn on Airplane Mode immediately to stop SMS OTP forwarding, then uninstall the malicious package.
  3. Preserve Uncropped Digital Evidence: Save screenshots of the 12-digit UPI UTR number, bank SMS alerts, WhatsApp/Telegram caller numbers, and chat logs.
  4. File Formal e-FIR & Notify Bank: Submit a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and hand the 14-digit acknowledgement receipt to your home bank branch within 24 hours.
