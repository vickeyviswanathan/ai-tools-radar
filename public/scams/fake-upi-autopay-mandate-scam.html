The Fake UPI Autopay / Mandate Approval Scam (UPI Mandate Fraud) — Modus Operandi & 1930 Protection Guide

Statutory citizen cyber defense advisory based on NPCI & RBI Digital Payment Safety guidelines.

📱 Attack Vector: UPI Autopay / Collect Request 🏷️ Pattern: UPI Mandate Fraud 🏛️ Source: NPCI & RBI Digital Payment Safety
NPCI Official Safety Info ↗ ← View All 31 Scam Alerts

🕵️ How the Scam Works (Modus Operandi)

⚠️ The Trap (UPI Autopay / Collect Request): Fraudsters pose as customer care, OTT/telecom billing teams, or marketplace buyers and send a UPI Autopay mandate request disguised as a 'refund approval', 'subscription cancellation', or 'KYC renewal'. When you enter your UPI PIN, you do not receive money—you authorise recurring automatic debits that quietly drain your bank account.

🛡️ Mandatory Defense Rule

✅ How to Protect Yourself & Your Family: Entering your UPI PIN is ALWAYS for paying or authorising a debit—never to receive a refund or cancel a plan. Regularly inspect the 'UPI Autopay / Mandates' section in your UPI app and revoke any unknown mandate.

1. Scam Overview

UPI Autopay was built by NPCI for legitimate recurring bills like OTT subscriptions, SIPs, and utility bills. Fraudsters abuse this feature by disguising an Autopay creation request as a 'refund credit', 'cashback claim', or 'auto-debit cancellation'. Because the prompt shows a small initial amount or misleading merchant name like 'Refund Desk', victims enter their UPI PIN and unknowingly lock in recurring debits.

2. The Initial Contact Simulated Educational Example

Channel: Phone Call + In-App UPI Collect / Mandate Notification · Note: The message below is a simulated educational example showing typical wording used by fraudsters.

[Illustrative Educational Example] Caller: "Hello, your annual OTT/antivirus plan was charged ₹4,999 by mistake. To cancel auto-renewal and receive your instant ₹4,999 refund, open your UPI app, tap the pending Autopay/Mandate notification, and enter your UPI PIN."

3. How the Scam Works (Step-by-Step)

  1. Pretext call or message: The scammer claims you were overcharged for a subscription, insurance policy, or failed order—or pretends to be a buyer sending an advance.
  2. Sending the mandate request: Instead of sending money, they trigger a UPI Autopay mandate creation request to your UPI ID.
  3. Deceptive instruction: They tell you that entering your UPI PIN 'completes the cancellation' or 'unlocks the refund credit'.
  4. PIN authorisation: Once you enter your UPI PIN, the mandate becomes active and allows scheduled or repeated debits up to the mandated cap without asking for your PIN again.

4. What the Victim Sees Simulated Screen — Educational Example

UPI App Mandate Approval Prompt (Simulated Educational Example)
UPI Autopay Request Merchant: DIGITAL-REFUND-SERVICES Frequency: As Presented / Weekly Max Mandate Amount: ₹15,000 Action Button: [Approve & Enter UPI PIN]

Notice the words 'Autopay Request' and 'Max Mandate Amount'—approving with a UPI PIN authorises money to leave your account, never to enter it.

5. The Psychology Behind It

Loss aversion and relief. Victims worried about losing ₹4,999 to an unwanted renewal are eager to 'cancel' quickly and overlook the mandate wording on their UPI screen.

6. Red Flags to Spot Immediately

  • Anyone claiming you must approve a UPI mandate or enter your UPI PIN to receive a refund or cancel a subscription.
  • Unfamiliar merchant names like 'Refund Desk', 'Customer Support', or personal-looking handles on an Autopay request.
  • Mandate requests showing 'Frequency: As Presented' or a high maximum amount (e.g. ₹15,000) for a small refund.

7. What Happens If the Victim Falls for It

  • Repeated unauthorised debits: Funds can be pulled automatically on schedule or 'as presented' until the mandate is revoked or the account is empty.
  • Delayed detection: Small recurring debits may go unnoticed for days if SMS alerts are missed.

8. How to Protect Yourself

  • Memorise the golden rule of UPI: You NEVER need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money or cancel a service.
  • Always read the full UPI prompt ('Pay to' vs 'Autopay Mandate') before touching the PIN pad.
  • Open your UPI app's 'Autopay / Mandates' menu monthly and pause or revoke anything you did not intentionally set up.

9. What to Do If Already Scammed

  1. Open your UPI app immediately, go to Autopay / Mandates, and REVOKE / DELETE the fraudulent mandate.
  2. Call your bank's fraud helpline to block further UPI debits if needed and check recent account statements.
  3. Dial 1930 immediately if any money has already been debited, and file a report at cybercrime.gov.in with the UPI Reference / UTR number.

10. India-Specific Reporting Channels

  • National Cyber Crime Helpline: Dial 1930 within the Golden Hour to freeze debited funds. (https://cybercrime.gov.in/)
  • NPCI UPI Dispute Portal: Raise an unauthorised UPI transaction or mandate dispute via your UPI app and NPCI. (https://www.npci.org.in/)
  • RBI Sachet / Banking Ombudsman: Escalate unresolved bank disputes through RBI's official grievance channels. (https://sachet.rbi.org.in/)

🚨 Emergency 4-Step Golden Hour Protocol (If Targeted)

  1. Dial 1930 Immediately (Golden Hour): Call the toll-free National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 within 2 hours of an unauthorized UPI/bank transfer to freeze the funds in the recipient mule account before ATM withdrawal.
  2. Disconnect Device if an APK Was Installed: If a file ending in .apk (e.g. e-Challan, Wedding Invite, KYC app) was installed, turn on Airplane Mode immediately to stop SMS OTP forwarding, then uninstall the malicious package.
  3. Preserve Uncropped Digital Evidence: Save screenshots of the 12-digit UPI UTR number, bank SMS alerts, WhatsApp/Telegram caller numbers, and chat logs.
  4. File Formal e-FIR & Notify Bank: Submit a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and hand the 14-digit acknowledgement receipt to your home bank branch within 24 hours.
