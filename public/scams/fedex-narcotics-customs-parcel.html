The FedEx / DHL "Drugs Found in International Courier" Threat (Customs Drug Parcel) — Modus Operandi & 1930 Protection Guide

Statutory citizen cyber defense advisory based on I4C & Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) Advisory guidelines.

📱 Attack Vector: Phone Call / Video Call 🏷️ Pattern: Customs Drug Parcel 🏛️ Source: I4C & Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) Advisory
National Cyber Helpline: 1930 ↗ ← View All 19 Scam Alerts

🕵️ How the Scam Works (Modus Operandi)

⚠️ The Trap (Phone Call / Video Call): An automated call claiming to be FedEx or DHL claims: "A courier parcel sent using your Aadhaar to Taiwan or Cambodia containing MDMA drugs, fake passports, and illegal debit cards was intercepted by customs." The call is transferred to fake "Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)" or "Mumbai Crime Branch" officers over video call who demand financial statements and emergency escrow transfers.

🛡️ Mandatory Defense Rule

✅ How to Protect Yourself & Your Family: Courier companies like FedEx and DHL never transfer customer calls to police or narcotics agencies. Police never interrogate suspects or demand security deposits over video calls. Hang up, block, and dial 1930.

🚨 Emergency 4-Step Golden Hour Protocol (If Targeted)

  1. Dial 1930 Immediately (Golden Hour): Call the toll-free National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 within 2 hours of an unauthorized UPI/bank transfer to freeze the funds in the recipient mule account before ATM withdrawal.
  2. Disconnect Device if an APK Was Installed: If a file ending in .apk (e.g. e-Challan, Wedding Invite, KYC app) was installed, turn on Airplane Mode immediately to stop SMS OTP forwarding, then uninstall the malicious package.
  3. Preserve Uncropped Digital Evidence: Save screenshots of the 12-digit UPI UTR number, bank SMS alerts, WhatsApp/Telegram caller numbers, and chat logs.
  4. File Formal e-FIR & Notify Bank: Submit a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and hand the 14-digit acknowledgement receipt to your home bank branch within 24 hours.
